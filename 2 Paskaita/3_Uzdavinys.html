Lasagna

Lasagna is a very versitile dish. It can be prepared with meat, poultry and can even be made vegan. The taste and texture is profound, it will grab you by the gut!

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta
  • Crushed tomatos
  • Meat, chicken or curd
  • A splash of love

How to make it

  1. Soak lasagna sheets in warm water.
  2. Spread tomato sause in a baking dish.
  3. Line lasagna sheets in the prepared baking dish.
  4. Add tomato sauce, cheese and other ingredients in layers with the lasagna sheets until the ingredients are used up.
